How to play

Wordle, backwards. You start with the answer. Pull it apart.

  • Tap a letter, type its replacement, press Enter. That's one move.
  • Reel (beta) drops the keyboard: spin any letter in the bottom row like a combination lock, then tap ✓.
  • Every move must be a real word. No repeats.
  • still where it started. a start letter that's come back somewhere. gone.
  • All grey wins. As many moves as you need. Fewer is better.
  • Par is the shortest way through in common words. Beat it and you've gone off the map.
  • Easy is the same game with four-letter words. Its own daily puzzle, its own stats.

One puzzle a day, new at midnight. Born in a family Wordle thread.
