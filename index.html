UNRAVEL

How to play

Wordle, backwards. You start with the answer. Pull it apart.

  • Change one letter per move. Five clean moves wins.
  • Every move must be a real word.
  • Letters in the start word are dead. You can't play them anywhere.
  • Each position can only be changed once.
  • Hit a dead end? The doomed rows go yellow, get wiped, and you rewind to your last good word.
  • As many moves as you need. Fewer is better. One per letter is perfect.
  • Easy is the same game with four-letter words. Its own daily puzzle, its own stats. Switch tabs any time; both games keep their place.

One puzzle a day, new at midnight. Every puzzle has at least one solution made of common words. Born in a family Wordle thread.
